Who I Am

About
Me

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Leadership and Management, Manav Rachna International Institute of Research Studies, Faridabad, with a deep academic grounding in Economics and Business Management.

My research sits at the crossroads of Ecotourism, FinTech, Machine Learning applications in Economics, and Sustainable Development — bridging traditional economic thought with data-driven innovation.

Beyond the classroom, I am a Professional Yoga Teacher and Practitioner — certified by the Indian Yoga Association at Kaivalyadhama, Lonavala — bringing mindfulness and holistic leadership into everything I do.
